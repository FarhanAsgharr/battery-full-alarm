Changelog

What changed, and when.

1.1.0

26 July 2026 Current

First build published as a downloadable APK.

Signing note. The 1.1.0 APKs are signed with a debug key, because no upload keystore exists yet. They install and run normally, but Android will refuse to update them from a properly-signed release later — that requires uninstalling first, which loses your settings and history.

Added

  • Remove any alarm sound. Every entry in the picker now has a Delete action. Sounds you imported or recorded are deleted from storage. The entries under On this device are your phone's own ringtones — deleting one would take it away from your clock app and every other alarm app, so those are hidden from the list instead, and the dialog says so.
  • Hiding survives an app restart and a device reboot.
  • Restore default sounds in Settings → Alarm, with a confirmation. It reports how many are hidden, and is disabled when there is nothing to restore.
  • Removing the sound currently set as your alarm resets the selection to your device default, so the alarm can never point at something that is gone.
  • Average charge gained now appears on the Statistics screen.
  • A proper adaptive app icon, replacing the Flutter placeholder.

Fixed — reliability

  • The alarm no longer loses timing accuracy after an hour: the wake lock is re-armed on every repeat, so Doze cannot stretch a 10-second interval into minutes.
  • Vibration and the torch are perceptible when both the sound and the spoken message are switched off. The burst used to begin and end in the same frame.
  • If your chosen alarm sound disappears, the alarm falls back to the device default rather than playing nothing.
  • A charging session left open by a killed process is closed at the last moment the battery was known to be charging, instead of reading "In progress" for ever.
  • A stale alarm notification left by a killed process is cleared on restart.
  • Monitoring is restored after an app update, not only after a reboot.
  • A JobScheduler watchdog restarts the monitor if an aggressive task-killer stops it — aimed at Xiaomi, Oppo, Realme, Vivo and Huawei.
  • Text-to-speech initialises on a worker thread; it was binding to the platform TTS service on the same thread Flutter draws on.
  • Long alarm tones and long messages are no longer cut short.

Fixed — privacy

  • Android cloud backup is now disabled. At its default it would have copied your settings, charging history and any recorded voice clip to your Google Drive, which contradicted the app's central claim.
  • If the app is killed while an alarm is ringing, your system alarm volume is restored on the next launch instead of staying where the app left it.

Fixed — interface

  • Leaving the alarm sound screen while the file picker or a permission dialog was open no longer crashes the app.
  • The volume and speech sliders save once, when you let go — not on every frame of a drag.
  • The announcement editor saves after a pause in typing, not per keystroke.
  • The battery gauge and statistics tiles no longer overflow at large system font sizes.
  • The ongoing notification is only re-posted when its visible text changes.

1.0.0

Internal Never published

The initial build. No APK was ever released for this version, so there is nothing to upgrade from.

  • Foreground service watching the system battery broadcast, alarming at 100% while plugged in and stopping the instant the charger is removed.
  • Repeating alarm chain: tone, spoken announcement, vibration and torch, then a wait of your chosen interval.
  • Custom announcement with name and battery-level tags, adjustable speech speed and pitch.
  • Alarm sound from the device ringtones, an imported file, or a clip recorded in the app.
  • Charging history and daily, weekly and monthly statistics, stored only on the device.
  • English, Urdu, Arabic and Hindi, with right-to-left layout.
  • Material 3 with light, dark and system themes.
