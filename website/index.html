What happens at 100%

The chain repeats at the interval you choose — 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds — and stops the instant the charger comes out.

  1. 01

    An alarm sounds

    Any alarm tone on your phone, an audio file you import, or a clip you record yourself. It plays on the alarm stream, so it is audible on silent.

  2. 02

    The phone speaks

    Your own sentence, read by the text-to-speech engine already on the device. Set your name, the wording, the speed and the pitch.

  3. 03

    It vibrates and flashes

    Vibration and the camera flash fire alongside the sound. Each can be turned off on its own.

  4. 04

    It waits, then does it again

    Silence for your chosen interval, then the whole chain repeats. A phone left charging does not get quietly ignored.

  5. 05

    You unplug — everything stops

    Sound, speech, vibration and torch cut immediately, the session is written to your local history, and monitoring re-arms for next time.

On the phone

Material 3, light and dark, in English, Urdu, Arabic and Hindi — right-to-left included.

What it doesn't do

This is the shorter and more interesting list. The app has no INTERNET permission, so most of these are enforced by Android rather than promised by us.

  • No network access The permission is not declared. It cannot reach the internet at all.
  • No account or sign-in Nothing to register, nothing to log into.
  • No analytics or ads No tracking SDKs of any kind are linked into the app.
  • No cloud backup Auto-backup is switched off, so your settings, history and recordings are never copied to Google Drive.
  • No location, contacts or files Seven permissions in total, two of them optional.
  • No subscription Free and open source under the MIT licence.
